# KrtLab — Venture-scale product strategy

## North star

KrtLab should become an outcomes platform that connects **career intent → demonstrated skills → trusted learning → verified capability → opportunity access**. It is not another course directory or generic AI chatbot.

A multi-million-dollar valuation is an outcome of durable business performance, not a feature checklist. This strategy defines the product and evidence required to earn that outcome; it does not promise a valuation.

## Initial market wedge

Start with one tightly defined customer problem: young people and early-career learners who do not know which skills to build, how to prove them, or how to find their next legitimate opportunity.

Launch with a focused geography and language segment where KrtLab can recruit learners and partner organizations directly. Validate Armenia first; expand only after repeatable acquisition, retention, learning outcomes and partner supply are demonstrated.

## Product architecture: one connected system

1. **Learner identity and portfolio** — a durable profile, goals, skill claims, evidence, project artifacts and consent controls.
2. **AI career mentor** — structured goal intake, clarifying questions, roadmap generation, revisions and explainable recommendations.
3. **Learning orchestration** — curated internal and external resources, practice, projects, checkpoints and adaptive next actions.
4. **Evidence and assessment** — rubric-based review, assessor identity, provenance, attempts, feedback and appeals. AI feedback is assistive, not an independent credential authority.
5. **Skills graph** — a canonical skill taxonomy linking goals, learning content, assessments, projects, courses and opportunities.
6. **Creator marketplace** — instructor onboarding, course proposals, moderation, learner reviews, refunds and later revenue share.
7. **Opportunity marketplace** — verified organizations, structured roles, eligibility, safeguarding, application tracking and explainable skill matching.
8. **Organization workspace** — partner dashboards, cohort management, approved learning pathways and aggregate outcomes without exposing private learner data.
9. **Trust and administration** — moderation queues, audit logs, reporting, abuse handling, data export/deletion and role-based access.

## Defensibility

The moat is not the language model. Models are replaceable. Defensibility must compound from:
- a normalized skills and competency graph;
- longitudinal, consented evidence of what learners can actually do;
- high-quality rubrics and assessment calibration;
- verified supply of instructors and opportunity providers;
- partner distribution through schools, NGOs and employers;
- outcome data connecting learning interventions to real progression.

Do not claim that user data is a moat unless it is lawfully collected, consented, secured and used in a privacy-preserving way.

## Revenue model hypotheses

Test willingness to pay before building complex billing:
- **B2C Plus:** advanced mentor workflows, portfolio analytics and structured learning plans.
- **B2B cohort license:** schools, NGOs and youth programs pay per active learner/cohort for mentor tools, assignments and outcome reporting.
- **Creator marketplace fee:** a transparent commission only after course quality, payments, refunds and tax/compliance operations are ready.
- **Employer/organization tools:** paid verified listings or talent-pipeline tools only after a credible learner supply and safeguarding process exist.

Keep core learner access useful without payment. Never charge for an unearned credential or imply accreditation that KrtLab does not hold.

## Operating metrics

Track the full funnel, segmented by acquisition channel and learner cohort:
- Activation: a learner completes goal intake and receives a usable roadmap.
- Time to first value: time from signup to first completed learning action.
- Weekly active learners and 4/8/12-week retention.
- Roadmap milestone completion and evidence submission rates.
- Assessment pass/retry rates and assessor agreement.
- Portfolio artifacts created and reviewed.
- Opportunity recommendation click-through, eligible applications and interview/outcome rates.
- Creator course approval, enrollment, completion, refund and repeat-purchase rates.
- B2B pilot renewal, seat utilization and partner-reported outcomes.
- Gross margin, customer acquisition cost, payback period and net revenue retention.

Set numeric targets only after a baseline cohort exists. Publish cohort definitions so metrics cannot be inflated by counting signups as active learners.

## Milestone gates

### Gate A — reliable learner loop
One goal → one structured roadmap → learning actions → evidence → feedback → revised next action. Reliable persistence, account isolation, deletion/export and mobile usability are mandatory.

### Gate B — prove learning outcomes
Run small supervised cohorts. Calibrate rubrics with human reviewers. Compare baseline and endline skill evidence. Fix weak retention before broad acquisition.

### Gate C — prove distribution and revenue
Secure repeatable school/NGO pilots, then test B2B renewal and a narrowly scoped paid learner plan. Do not build a large marketplace before supply and demand are both present.

### Gate D — marketplace liquidity
Launch one category and one geography at a time. Verify every organization, moderate every listing, disclose eligibility and compensation, and measure completed matches rather than raw listings.

### Gate E — international expansion
Localize language, curriculum, legal terms, safeguarding, payments and partner operations. Expansion is earned by repeatable unit economics, not by adding flags or translations.

## Non-negotiable platform requirements

- Server-side authorization for privileged actions; never trust a role field editable by the client.
- Tenant isolation for organizations and cohort data.
- Versioned schemas and non-destructive migrations with backups and rollback.
- Server-side AI calls, quotas, rate limits, abuse controls and structured output validation.
- Audit trails for assessment, moderation, credentials and organization verification.
- Privacy-by-design: collect minimum data, define retention, provide access/export/deletion and avoid exposing minors to unverified contacts or unsuitable roles.
- Accessibility, localization, responsive performance and observable error handling.
- No fabricated courses, employers, reviews, credentials, outcomes or match probabilities.

## Valuation discipline

A valuation cannot be engineered directly. It is negotiated from growth, retention, revenue quality, margins, market size, defensibility, team execution and risk. KrtLab should optimize for a product people repeatedly use and organizations renew—not for a headline valuation.
